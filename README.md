# BarCo Supply Chain Optimisation

## Project Overview

This project analyses BarCo Corporation’s production and distribution planning under natural gas curtailment. The aim was to use linear programming and transportation modelling to recommend how limited gas supply should be allocated across products and plants while reducing the impact on daily profit.

[View full supply chain optimisation report](BarCoSupplyChainReport.pdf)

## Business Problem

BarCo faces natural gas supply cuts from MaxEnergy, its only supplier. These cuts affect both production and distribution, creating a need for a clear decision-making model to determine which products should continue, which should be reduced, and how gas should be distributed across plants.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Production planning under 20% and 40% gas curtailment

- Transportation cost optimisation
- Shadow price and sensitivity analysis
- Sustainability-oriented production scenario

## Key Findings

- Under a 20% gas cut, daily profit decreases from $612,850 to $530,605.
- Under a 40% gas cut, daily profit decreases to $426,800.
- The recommended distribution routes remain the same across curtailment scenarios.
- The most stable high-priority products are Ammonium Phosphate, Urea, Chlorine, and Ammonium Nitrate.
- VCM, Caustic Soda, and Ammonium Nitrate require monitoring because their rankings are sensitive to profit changes.
- A sustainability-oriented plan that prioritises Ammonia can improve both profit and emissions outcomes.

## Business Recommendations

- Use the LP plan before accepting supplier-driven production cuts.
- Keep the top four products running at full capacity where possible.
- Use VCM and Caustic Soda as buffer products during curtailment.
- Maintain the same optimal distribution routing pattern.
- Re-run the optimisation model regularly as prices, demand, and gas availability change.
